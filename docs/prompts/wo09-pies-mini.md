## What it is
A basket ("pie") is one of our own fixed lists of stocks. **Binance returns no baskets** (its `tabId` filters are ignored, `IDEAS.md` §Findings): the baskets are templates in the repo. The user enters a budget in USDT and a weight for each token, and Tally buys the tokens one after another through ShareGuard, one wallet confirmation sequence per token, with no manual picking. Out of scope and shown as roadmap: atomic one-transaction baskets, batch buying, automatic rebalancing, selling a basket.
