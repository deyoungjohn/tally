# Portfolio and statements

## Holdings in shares

The Portfolio page reads a wallet's tokenized-stock balances from the chain and adds them up in **shares** across issuers. 1.2 shares from Ondo and 0.5 from bStock read as 1.7 shares of the stock, not two token balances. Anyone can paste any address to look at it; the data is public chain data.

* A token whose multiplier cannot be read is shown as unknown and excluded from share totals. It has no Sell button.
* Balances under $1 are left out of the main list and open in a dialog.
* Dividends show as shares gained: for Ondo the multiplier grows, so the same token count is worth more shares.

## Statements

A worker builds a statement per signed-in wallet and stores it as a snapshot: holdings, activity and realized or unrealized gain where the data supports it. **Realized gain is shown only where it is known.** A missing cost basis says "unknown", never zero.

The Statement tab exports a CSV from the same view model that draws the screen, so the numbers match. A PDF export (with a clickable "Generated by Tally" footer line) is queued.

## Activity

Activity lists the wallet's guarded buys, sells and Migrates with their status and a BscScan link, read from the receipts module.

## Suggestions for small portfolios

A wallet that holds fewer than three stocks sees suggestions: three minus the number held. It is not a recommendation engine. Candidates are tokens enabled on ShareGuard that Radar grades A or B (Liquid), not ghost and with fresh data, ordered by cleaned 24-hour on-chain volume, then rotated per wallet by a stable hash of the address so different wallets see a different token first. While the wallet's holdings or the Radar data are still loading, it says so and suggests nothing. Smart recommendations are on the [roadmap](../reference/roadmap.md).

## Speed

Portfolio inspects only the tickers the wallet holds, caches the wallet-independent part for 30 seconds and runs at most four reads at a time. The first version walked every supported ticker one by one, which grew from 6 to 21 tickers and made the page take 15 to 20 seconds. See [Challenges](../building-tally/challenges.md#a-page-that-got-slower-as-the-list-got-longer).
